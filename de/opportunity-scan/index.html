Start With Clarity

Understand the opportunity before investing in the solution.

New equipment may be part of the answer. But the strongest opportunity could also be found in layout, guest experience, positioning or the way the space is used.

We look first. Then we decide what is actually worth improving.

What We Look At

One gym. Six important perspectives.

The scan connects fitness quality with the wider guest and business experience.

01
Fitness Capability

Can guests train the way they expect?

Workout possibilities, training balance and missing essentials.

02
Equipment

Is the equipment supporting the experience?

Condition, suitability, gaps and replacement priorities.

03
Guest Experience

Does the gym match the quality of the stay?

Atmosphere, usability, presentation and guest guidance.

04
Space & Flow

Is every square metre working effectively?

Layout, training zones, movement, capacity and unused space.

05
Positioning

Does the fitness offer fit the property?

Guest expectations, differentiation and brand alignment.

06
Business Opportunity

Could the gym create more value?

Utilisation, future development and relevant commercial opportunities.
