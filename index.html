The Fitness Experience

The gym should not feel like an extra room. It should feel like part of the stay.

A fitness space influences more than a workout. It contributes to how guests experience a property, how a brand positions itself and how much potential a facility creates.

TopsGyms looks beyond individual pieces of equipment to identify where the entire fitness experience can become stronger.

01 Experience

Does the space match the experience your guests expect?

From first impression to usability, atmosphere and workout possibilities, every detail contributes to how the fitness facility is perceived.

02 Performance

Can guests actually train the way they want to?

Missing essentials, outdated equipment or an inefficient layout can limit what should be a complete fitness experience.

03 Business

Is your gym creating all the value it could?

A stronger fitness offering can support guest satisfaction, positioning, utilisation and new commercial opportunities.
