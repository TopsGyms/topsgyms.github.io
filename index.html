07 — FAQ

Before we start.

Short answers to the questions that matter before a first conversation.

Is TopsGyms a wellness consultancy?+

No. TopsGyms’ primary focus is advancing hotel and resort gyms. If a client later wants adjacent wellness elements assessed, that can be scoped separately where it supports the project, but it is not the core service.

Do we need to be planning a full renovation?+

No. The value can be in improving an existing gym, planning a larger upgrade, preparing a new opening or simply deciding where the next investment should go.

Does advancement automatically mean spending more?+

No. The scan is designed to identify priorities first. Some opportunities may be operational, experiential or communication-led; others may justify a larger investment.

What happens after the Gym Opportunity Scan?+

You receive a prioritized direction and advancement roadmap. If useful, TopsGyms can then support the next phase around strategy, concept choices and implementation decisions.

What if our gym is already strong?+

That can be an excellent starting point. The question then becomes how to sharpen differentiation, improve consistency and make the gym work harder for the guest and property.

Can the work be done remotely or on-site?+

The right format depends on the property and scope. A first conversation establishes what information is available and whether remote analysis, an on-site review or a combination is most appropriate.
