What We Look At

One gym. Six important perspectives.

Le scan relie la qualité fitness à l’expérience globale des clients et de l’activité.

01
Fitness Capability

Can guests train the way they expect?

Workout possibilities, training balance and missing essentials.

02
Equipment

Is the equipment supporting the experience?

Condition, suitability, gaps and replacement priorities.

03
Expérience client

La salle de fitness est-elle à la hauteur du séjour ?

Atmosphere, usability, presentation and guest guidance.

04
Space & Flow

Is every square metre working effectively?

Layout, training zones, movement, capacity and unused space.

05
Positioning

Does the fitness offer fit the property?

Attentes des clients, différenciation et cohérence avec la marque.

06
Opportunité commerciale

Could the gym create more value?

Utilisation, future development and relevant commercial opportunities.
