The TopsGyms Principle

Look first. Improve second.

The goal is not to change as much as possible. It is to understand where improvement can genuinely make the gym stronger.

01

Keep what already works.

02

Identify what limits the experience.

03

Invest where it can create the most value.

Meaningful advancement. Not unnecessary change.

The Advancement Process

Five clear steps.

Every step keeps the guest, the property and the purpose behind the investment connected.

01 Discover

Understand the current situation.

Before making recommendations, we first understand the gym and the property around it.

Current fitness offering
Guest and property needs
Challenges, ambitions and planned investment
Outcome

A clear picture of where the strongest opportunities may be.

02 Analyse

Look beyond the obvious.

Equipment is only one part of the fitness experience.

Fitness

Can guests train effectively?

Experience

Does the gym match the stay?

Space

Is the layout working efficiently?

Business

Is the gym creating the value it could?

Outcome

A connected view of what is working and what could become stronger.

03 Prioritise

Focus on what matters most.

Not every opportunity deserves the same attention or investment.

Highest-impact improvements
Practical priorities
Logical order of action
Outcome

Clear priorities instead of an unnecessarily long wish list.

04 Advance

Turn direction into improvement.

When the right direction is clear, TopsGyms can help move it towards reality.

Equipment and layout decisions
Concept and experience development
Implementation support
Outcome

Practical advancement rather than recommendations that stay on paper.

05 Support

Keep the gym moving forward.

Fitness spaces continue to change after an upgrade is complete.

Future equipment decisions
Periodic improvement opportunities
Ongoing strategic support
Outcome

A fitness partner you can return to as needs and opportunities evolve.
