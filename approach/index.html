Our Approach

Advancement starts with understanding.

Every fitness space is different. Different property. Different guests. Different ambitions. Different opportunities.

That is why TopsGyms does not begin with a standard package or predefined solution. We first understand the current situation, identify what matters most and build the right path forward from there.

The TopsGyms Principle

Look first. Improve second.

It is easy to look at a gym and immediately start suggesting new equipment, a different layout or a complete refurbishment.

But without understanding the reason behind those changes, investment can quickly become disconnected from what the property actually needs.

TopsGyms approaches advancement differently.

01

Understand what already works.

02

Identify what limits the current fitness offering.

03

Understand what guests should be able to experience.

04

Consider how the gym fits within the wider property.

05

Identify where investment could create the greatest impact.

06

Determine what should happen first.

The objective is not maximum change. It is meaningful advancement.

A Deliberate Approach

Not every gym needs more.

01

We do not recommend change for the sake of change.

If an element already works well, there is no reason to replace it simply because something newer exists.

02

We do not force properties into identical packages.

A boutique hotel and a large resort may require completely different solutions.

03

We do not focus only on appearance.

A gym should look strong, but it must also function for the people using it.

04

We do not separate advice from reality.

Recommendations need to consider practical implementation, available space and the ambitions of the property.

Better does not always mean bigger. It means more appropriate.

Europe-First

Look internationally. Apply intelligently.

Fitness and hospitality continue to evolve across Europe and beyond.

New concepts, technologies, training preferences and guest expectations can create valuable opportunities.

But simply copying what works somewhere else does not automatically make sense for another property.

01

Observe internationally

Follow relevant developments across fitness, hospitality and wellness.

02

Evaluate strategically

Determine whether a development has genuine relevance for the property.

03

Translate locally

Adapt useful ideas to the property, its guests and its market.

International insight only becomes valuable when it works locally.
