About TopsGyms

Built to advance more than the gym.

TopsGyms was created around a simple belief: a fitness space should contribute to more than the ability to work out.

It should strengthen the experience of the property, support its positioning and create value for the business around it.

Why TopsGyms

A gym can be more than a facility.

For many hotels and resorts, the fitness space is still treated as an additional room: equipment is placed inside, the door is opened and the gym is considered complete.

But guest expectations are changing.

Fitness, wellness and health are becoming increasingly relevant within the wider travel experience. Guests notice whether a gym feels considered, whether they can actually complete the workout they want and whether the quality of the facility matches the rest of the property.

That creates an opportunity to turn fitness from something a property simply has into something that actively contributes to the wider stay and business.

Guest experience
Positioning
Perceived quality

Business value
The gym should not feel like an extra room. It should feel like part of the stay.

Meet the Founder

The perspective behind TopsGyms.

TopsGyms was founded by Sofie Tops from a strong connection with sport and a growing fascination with the relationship between fitness experience and business performance.

With a background in Sportkunde and practical experience within sport, training and fitness environments, Sofie developed an understanding of what happens where it matters most: on the training floor.

That perspective became the foundation for something broader.

Because improving a gym is not only about knowing how someone trains. It also requires understanding the property, its guests and what the fitness offering should contribute.
