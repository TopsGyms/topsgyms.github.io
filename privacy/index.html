Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TopsGyms collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us or request a Gym Opportunity Scan.

Last updated: 2 September 2026 Applies to: topsgyms.com

On this page

TopsGyms respects your privacy. We only aim to collect information that is relevant to operating the website, responding to business enquiries and exploring potential work with hotels, resorts and other fitness-related organisations.

01

Who is responsible for your data?

TopsGyms is the controller responsible for personal information processed through this website.

TopsGyms

Netherlands

Email: sofie@topsgyms.com

02

What information may we collect?

The information we process depends on how you interact with TopsGyms.

Information you provide

  • Your name
  • Your business email address
  • Your role or job title
  • Your company, hotel, resort or property name
  • Your location
  • Your website
  • Information about your fitness facility
  • Your objectives, challenges or planned investments
  • The contents of messages you send to TopsGyms
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

Technical information

When a website is accessed, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the infrastructure used to deliver the website. This may include an IP address, browser information, device information, timestamps and requested pages.

03

Why do we use personal information?

TopsGyms may process personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Reviewing Gym Opportunity Scan requests
  • Arranging introductory conversations
  • Understanding a potential client's property, fitness space and requirements
  • Preparing or discussing potential services or projects
  • Maintaining relevant business correspondence
  • Protecting the website and forms against misuse or spam
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

Legal bases

Depending on the situation, TopsGyms relies on one or more lawful bases under the GDPR.

  • Steps prior to entering into a contract: where processing is necessary to respond to a request relating to potential services or cooperation.
  • Legitimate interests: including responding to relevant business enquiries, operating and protecting the website and developing legitimate business relationships.
  • Consent: where you have specifically given consent for a particular use of your information.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is required by applicable law.
04

Website forms and Formspree

TopsGyms uses Formspree to process submissions from the Contact form and Gym Opportunity Scan form.

When you submit one of these forms, the information entered into the form is transmitted to Formspree so that the submission can be processed and delivered to TopsGyms.

Formspree acts as a service provider involved in processing form submissions. Formspree states that it supports GDPR compliance and relies on Standard Contractual Clauses where relevant for international processing.

Form submissions may also be stored within the TopsGyms Formspree account according to the account configuration and Formspree's applicable retention arrangements.

TopsGyms does not use information submitted through these forms to automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

You can review Formspree's own privacy information at Formspree's Privacy Policy .

05

Hosting and technical services

GitHub Pages

The TopsGyms website is hosted using GitHub Pages. As part of delivering the website, hosting and network infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser request data.

Google Fonts

This website currently loads font files through Google Fonts. When these resources are requested, the visitor's browser connects to Google infrastructure. Technical information such as an IP address may therefore be processed by Google.

TopsGyms does not use Google Fonts for advertising or behavioural profiling.

Google's privacy information is available through Google's Privacy Policy .

06

Who may receive your information?

TopsGyms does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers where this is necessary to operate the website or respond to your request. At present these may include:

  • Formspree, for website form processing
  • GitHub Pages and related hosting infrastructure
  • Google, where Google Fonts resources are requested
  • Email and communication providers used by TopsGyms

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a court order or another legally binding obligation.

07

International data transfers

Some service providers used by TopsGyms operate internationally or may process information outside the European Economic Area.

Where applicable, transfers should be supported by a lawful transfer mechanism such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Formspree states that it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses as a data processor for GDPR purposes.

08

How long do we keep information?

TopsGyms aims not to retain personal information for longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Business enquiries and related correspondence may generally be retained for up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact where this is useful for following up a business relationship, unless:

  • a shorter period is appropriate;
  • you request deletion and no overriding reason requires retention;
  • a longer period is required for legal, accounting or contractual purposes.

Third-party processors may apply their own technical retention periods to information they process on behalf of TopsGyms.

09

Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access your personal information
  • The right to correct inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to object to certain processing
  • The right to data portability where applicable
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise a privacy right, contact sofie@topsgyms.com .

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

More information is available at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl .

10

Security

TopsGyms takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. TopsGyms therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

11

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, TopsGyms services, legal requirements or the technology used by the website changes.

The latest version will always be published on this page together with its last-updated date.
