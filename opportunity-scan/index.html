Gym Opportunity Scan

What could your gym do better?

Every fitness space has opportunities. Some are immediately visible. Others only become clear when equipment, guest experience, positioning, operations and business potential are looked at together.

The TopsGyms Gym Opportunity Scan creates a structured view of where your fitness offering stands today — and where it could go next.

Before the Solution

We do not start by selling equipment. We start by understanding the opportunity.

Replacing equipment might be the answer. But it might not be the first answer.

Perhaps the workout offering is incomplete. The layout may be limiting the space. The fitness experience may not match the rest of the property. Guests may not know what is available. Or a facility with significant potential may simply be underused.

That is why TopsGyms looks before it recommends.

The goal of the scan is not to find something to sell. It is to find what is worth improving.

The Complete Fitness Picture

One gym. Multiple perspectives.

A fitness space cannot be properly assessed from one perspective alone. The Gym Opportunity Scan connects how the space performs for the guest with how it supports the property around it.

01
Fitness Capability

Can guests complete the workouts they expect?

We consider equipment coverage, training possibilities, missing essentials, balance between training types and practical usability.

02
Equipment & Condition

Is the equipment supporting or limiting the experience?

We look at suitability, condition, age, duplication, gaps and areas where replacement or expansion may deserve attention.

03
Guest Experience

Does the gym feel like part of the property?

We consider first impression, atmosphere, accessibility, guidance, presentation and connection to the wider stay.

04
Space & Flow

Is the available space being used effectively?

Layout, training zones, movement, capacity and inefficient areas are considered together.

05
Positioning & Business

Is the fitness offering creating the value it could?

We explore positioning, differentiation, utilisation, guest expectations and commercial opportunity.

06
Future Opportunity

What could become relevant next?

We consider ambitions, changing expectations and relevant developments in fitness, hospitality and technology.
